## The stacking rules that decide whether your code actually works

Walgreens Photo's coupon terms allow more stacking than most stores — with limits that trip people up.

Per order, you can combine **up to three coupons or strike-through sale discounts** in the online cart. Per item, it's one code or one sale price, never both. That means you could run OCTCARD on cards and COZY10 on a mug in the same checkout, which is a legitimately good setup for holiday ordering. What you can't do is stack two codes on the same canvas print.

Three rules from the terms are less obvious:

1. **Account product credits get applied first — and they block codes.** If you have photo credits sitting in your Walgreens account from a previous promotion, the system applies them before any coupon, and credits can't be combined with coupon codes or sale pricing. If your total looks weirdly low but no code seemed to fire, this is usually why.
2. **Discounts don't touch taxes, shipping, or past purchases.** The percentage comes off product prices only.
3. **Same-day doesn't mean guaranteed-same-day.** Walgreens says pickup time is an estimate; rely on the order status notifications.

One more account-level thing: coupon clipping on Walgreens.com requires a registered myWalgreens account. Without it, codes won't apply at checkout. It's free, so there's no reason to skip it — and myWalgreens members also earn 1% back sitewide (5% on Walgreens-owned brands) on top of everything else.
